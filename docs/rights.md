# Rights, publication and download safety / Telif ve güvenlik

## Know what each classification means

| Class | Interpretation | Publication action |
|---|---|---|
| `conditional` | Platform or asset class may permit reuse | Read the current license and item-specific restrictions |
| `item` | Asset-level rights vary | Inspect original item and license; do not publish if uncertain |
| `paid_license` | License purchase/plan may grant rights | Obtain license covering project, channel, geography and term |
| `restricted` | Learning/reference or noncommercial limits | Obtain separate permission for intended use if needed |
| `discovery` | Scene/quote/reference discovery, no grant established | Do not republish merely because it can be watched or exported |
| `tool` | Create your own media | Check input media, templates, output and plan terms |

Free access, royalty-free, open access, public domain, Creative Commons and commercial-use permission are different things. “Royalty-free” can require purchase and may impose conditions. A media site's safety cannot be guaranteed, and no file in this catalog has been scanned.

## Creative Commons and public domain

- **CC0 / public domain:** often reusable without copyright attribution; verify applicable country, underlying works, restoration, music and related rights. Credit provenance even if not mandatory.
- **CC BY:** credit creator, title where supplied, source, license and changes; follow license conditions.
- **CC BY-SA:** adaptation must comply with the applicable share-alike terms; check the distinction between an adaptation and a collection.
- **CC BY-NC:** do not assume monetized, sponsored or business videos qualify; obtain suitable permission.
- **CC BY-ND:** no distribution of adapted material under this license; cutting, dubbing, syncing music or other edits may raise issues. Check the particular license and law.
- **RightsStatements.org labels:** informational statements are not themselves licenses; trace to the original institution.

Official references: [Creative Commons licenses](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/use-remix/cc-licenses/), [RightsStatements.org](https://rightsstatements.org/), [Wikimedia reuse](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:REUSE), [Europeana rights](https://pro.europeana.eu/page/available-rights-statements).

## Specific traps

- Famous movie, anime, TV and meme scenes remain copyrighted unless a valid license or legally applicable exception permits use. There is no universal 3, 5, 10 or 30-second rule. Editing, cropping, changing speed or adding voiceover does not itself give permission. Do not use a downloader to evade platform terms or rights.
- For German/EU context, quotation, parody, caricature and pastiche exceptions may apply depending on the actual use. See the German [UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/) and EU [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/); for Türkiye, see [Mevzuat](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/). This is general information, not legal advice.
- US public domain does not automatically establish public-domain status worldwide. Government media can include partner assets, logos, endorsements and copyrighted soundtracks. Check original item. NASA visual and audio tracks can have separate rights ([guidance](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/)).
- Pexels and Pixabay permit many commercial uses under custom licenses; no blanket grant for identifiable people, trademarks, misleading endorsements or standalone redistribution. [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) · [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).
- Mixkit has both Free and Restricted stock-video licenses; [read terms](https://mixkit.co/license/). Regular HTML PhET simulations are currently CC BY-NC 4.0, including screenshot/video restrictions ([licensing](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/licensing/)).
- Openverse indexes images and audio (not a native video search); an index entry is not evidence of uploader authority. Smithsonian CC0 applies to marked items. OWID's original work is permissively licensed but third-party data can differ.
- Music: separately consider composition, recording, sync, territory, distribution and Content ID; keep license evidence. YouTube's Audio Library guidance is [here](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882). No directory can guarantee claim-free publication.
- For people, children, medical cases, brands, private property, sensitive locations and cultural heritage: check privacy, releases, personality rights, dignity and other restrictions beyond copyright. Avoid misrepresenting real people or events.

## Asset checklist (TR/EN)

1. Original asset URL and author/publisher; confirm original publication rather than repost. / Özgün kaynak ve hak sahibi.
2. Exact license/version, evidence URL, date and scope (commercial, edits, audio, territories). / Lisans ve kullanım kapsamı.
3. Save invoice, download receipt or local license snapshot if appropriate; do not republish copyrighted documentation. / Kanıtı sakla.
4. Confirm release/privacy, logos, music and third-party elements. / Kişi, marka, müzik hakları.
5. Attribute according to the license, including modifications. / Gerekli atıf ve değişiklik bilgisi.
6. If unclear, find an alternative or get written permission; use `data/asset-manifest-template.json` for records. / Belirsizse izin al veya alternatif seç.

Example attribution: “Title — Creator, Source URL, CC BY 4.0 (license URL); cropped and colour-adjusted.” Follow the original license's exact requirements.

## Download hygiene

Prefer official provider download buttons and verified HTTPS domains; avoid ad lookalikes, fake play buttons, unsolicited ZIP files, executables, browser extensions from unofficial stores and codec-install prompts. Media extensions such as MP4, MOV, WEBM or GIF are not a guarantee of safety. Keep OS, browser and media applications updated; scan local files with trusted security software, use hash reputation when appropriate, and don't upload confidential or unreleased material to public scanning sites. Check file type and size before opening, sandbox unusual files, and never run scripts embedded in a download. The repo contains links and original text/code, not third-party media files.
